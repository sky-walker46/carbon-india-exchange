Problem

Small climate projects face enterprise-sized barriers.

A farmer adopting improved land management, a dairy cooperative capturing methane or a rural enterprise converting waste may create genuine climate value. Turning that activity into a verified carbon asset is much harder.

FragmentationMillions of small assets are too costly to certify one by one.
MeasurementCredits require baselines, monitoring, uncertainty treatment and evidence.
CertificationRegistries, VVBs and methodology reviews create specialist work and fixed costs.
TrustBuyers need proof of additionality, ownership, serials and retirement.
